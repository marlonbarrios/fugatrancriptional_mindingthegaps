## ⚠️ **Important Notes**

### Performance Variations
- **Script Complexity**: Languages with complex scripts (Arabic, Thai, Devanagari) may have formatting issues
- **Dialectal Variations**: AI performs best with standard/formal variants
- **Cultural Context**: Some cultural nuances may be missed or misrepresented
- **Technical Terminology**: Performance varies significantly for specialized vocabulary

### Languages NOT Included
The following language types are **NOT** reliably supported:
- Most indigenous languages (Cherokee, Navajo, Quechua, Maya, etc.)
- Constructed languages except Esperanto (Klingon, Elvish, Dothraki)
- Most African languages beyond the 9 listed
- Most Pacific island languages beyond the 4 listed
- Historical/dead languages except Latin and Sanskrit
- Sign languages (ASL, BSL, etc.)
- Most minority/regional languages with limited training data

### Recommendations for Use
1. **Tier 1-2 languages**: Reliable for all applications
2. **Tier 3 languages**: Good for basic content, verify complex topics
3. **Tier 4-5 languages**: Basic content only, cultural sensitivity required
4. **Tier 6 languages**: Specialized use cases only
